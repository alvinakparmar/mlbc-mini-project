How Perceptual Hash Thresholds Work

Hamming Distance ≤ 5:

High visual similarity. The images are likely identical or modified only by resolution downscaling, WebP/JPG compression, or subtle color grade shifts.

Hamming Distance 6 - 10:

Moderate visual similarity. The images share structure but have experienced cropping, watermarking, filters, or rotation tweaks.

Hamming Distance > 10:

Visually distinct images. High bit difference indicates separate subjects or structural redesign.
